# Four-file contract

In this project the four files are generated by `scripts/build_four_files.py` from one source file (see
[production.md](production.md)); the contract below is what the generator guarantees. Screenplay beats use
`**NNN · mm:ss.s–mm:ss.s** — …` + `*Narrator:*` lines (read by doc-edit `story.py brief`); the Visual_Edit_Plan adds an
intro map **one row per second**, a footage status table and a cut map of every shot.

## Naming and structure

One film directory holds only its four final files. The slug is descriptive and stable. Keep temporary research, generation scripts and validation reports outside the deliverable directory.

### 1. Film_Slug_Screenplay_EN.md

Use:

- Title, target and central question/answer.
- Runtime map with intro, each story and ending; times span the full target without overlaps.
- Geography and production premise: observed footage vs illustrative reconstruction.
- Research anchors with working primary-source URLs and limitations.
- **Animal bible**: ID, species, adult male/female/unknown, young category/sex if known, identity markers, permitted continuity, one-line metaphor.
- Timed screenplay: scene/beat number and start–end; geography, camera distance/direction, what the viewer sees and learns; *Animals / sex / young*; sound/musical turn; narrator text copied from the transcript.
- Alternate edit and revised voice for shots whose outcomes lack supporting footage. Do not preserve a kill line over a non-kill clip.
- Mix and factual QA gates.

Use scene spans for visual planning; they are not narrator onsets. A 60–90 second span normally contains several shots, not one long static image. Explain discontinuities between locations/days in the visible edit.

### 2. transcript.md

Contains **English narrator text only** in story/scene order. No Markdown title, timestamps, numbering, character labels, scene labels, visual notes or sound directions. A nonblank physical line is **one audio-generation voice block** and one timestamp entry. A block may contain two or three naturally joined sentences if the chosen voice engine can render it coherently; break long blocks at meaningful emotion/information boundaries. Keep a blank line between scenes. If a scene has two voice blocks, place its two nonblank lines together and then one blank line.

Example of two scenes and three voice blocks:

    The herd is still feeding when the first shadow crosses the grass.
    One calf sees it too late to reach the middle.

    The adults turn back. The open path is now filled with bodies.

Do not put a sentence on a separate line merely because of punctuation if it is part of the same intended voice block. Conversely, if two onsets are needed, make two nonblank lines and two timestamp entries. This combines natural paragraphs with exact line synchronization.

### 3. Film_Slug_Timestamp_EN_VI.md

Use only the heading, a short note that onsets are planned until audio conformance, and two sections:

    ## English

    [00:00.6] The herd is still feeding when the first shadow crosses the grass.
    [00:09.8] One calf sees it too late to reach the middle.

    ## Vietnamese

    [00:00.6] Cả đàn vẫn ăn cỏ khi chiếc bóng đầu tiên lướt qua.
    [00:09.8] Một bê con nhìn thấy quá muộn để chạy vào giữa.

The English text after the bracket must be **byte-for-byte identical** to the corresponding nonblank transcript line. The Vietnamese entry translates the entire block without shortening or adding unrelated narration. Same count, same ordered onsets, and full section through the last line. A timestamp marks the start of a spoken block, never a pause or audio event. Do not write [Pause 6 seconds], [crickets], SFX, shot IDs or music in either timestamp section. Pauses are inferred from the onset spacing and described in the other two files.

Default precision: [mm:ss.s] including [00:00.6]. If a film exceeds 99:59, switch to [hh:mm:ss.s] consistently and adapt the validator.

### 4. Film_Slug_Visual_Edit_Plan_EN.md

Use:

- Film slug/runtime and thesis.
- Matching animal bible including male/female/young and each one-line metaphor.
- Cold-open cut map: 00:00–00:15 gentle animals and landscape under soft music (no drums), 00:15–01:15 naturally rising drum track and coherent strong shots, 01:15–01:30 breath/central question.
- Track map: A2 narrator; A3 and A4 ambience; A5 actual natural sound; A6 background music; A7 and A8 restrained SFX.
- Beat table: number/time, clip requirements, species/sex/age and direction, story function, source/continuity gate, audio and optional fallback. Note where voice yields to action.
- Clip sourcing log specification: clip ID, license, location, exact species, count, age/sex evidence, action and true outcome, duration, direction, light, rights and usable sound.
- Final cut gates and replacements if footage unavailable.

## Cross-file invariant

Number of nonblank transcript lines = number of [onset] English lines = number of [onset] Vietnamese lines. Their ordered timestamps are identical across languages. Screenplay narrator text and visual plan must agree with the final voice. If any line is rewritten, regenerate its translation and validate all three files again.
