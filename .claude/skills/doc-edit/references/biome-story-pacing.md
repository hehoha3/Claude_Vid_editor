# Biome story pacing (doc-edit plan)

Editing hints for the shot plan, chosen by spec header `# BIOME savanna|rainforest`. They order and pace shots;
they never supply species, events, humidity, colour or sound effects: those come from the screenplay and the footage.
Congo = `rainforest` but only Congo species, places and sounds (no jaguar / caiman in Africa, no Amazon LUT on savanna).
Hook rules, headers and rhythm: `edit-grammar.md`.

## Hook first (both biomes)
- First 10 s: one visible event (a turn, prey leaving frame, a rival arriving, a short standoff). Then, by 10-20 s,
  a wide / medium that shows predator, prey, distance and way out. Start at the conflict and step back in time with a
  clear transition if needed. By 1:30: central question, main animal, current risk, 2-3 images promising later stories;
  no spoilers.
- A hook that cannot be built from the footage = `THIẾU` in the report, never another animal standing in.

## savanna (open country, herds, face-offs)
- Space does the work: wide to place the herd, the distance, the cover and the escape line; medium for the decision;
  telephoto tracking for the run. Keep one screen direction through a chase.
- Beats: herd settles -> one animal separates or a rival closes -> distance shrinks -> contact or miss -> 4-8 s of
  aftermath. Dust, heat and natural daylight stay natural; no automatic golden-orange.
- Drama is the cost of a choice (keep the kill or protect the young; push on or avoid the horns), not a throne or a
  revenge story the screenplay does not have.

## rainforest (cover, approach, short bursts)
- Concealment does the work: slow approach through foliage (holds of 3-5 s), a visible reaction, then a short burst.
  Show what hides the threat before the animal notices it; a medium that shows the route beats a tight shot.
- Beats: sound or movement in cover -> sighting -> reaction -> burst (1-2 s cuts only if the movement path reads) ->
  quiet reaction shot. Keep leaf green and shadow detail natural; not everything blue-black.

## Small examples (patterns, not content)
- Hook: `1 | [L] Sư tử lao theo bầy trâu | real | sp=lion act=run size=medium dir=left` (event in frame 1) ->
  `3 | [L][B] Wide: sư tử, trâu, bụi cây chắn lối thoát | real | sp=lion,buffalo size=wide` (placing shot).
- Story turn: mark the real change of advantage with `# TURN` under that `## N`; one per 45-75 s is plenty.
- Pause: after a contact, 4-8 s of one reaction shot with the natural bed (A6/A3), music lowered or off only if that
  helps hear it; no SFX unless the source sound matches.
